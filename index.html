About me

I've handled the big,
chaotic, high-stakes ones

"Now I bring that same calm professionalism to the day that matters most to you — that's the Lou promise."

With a background in large-scale corporate events — managing gatherings of up to 500 people — I know how to keep things moving when the stakes are high and the timeline is tight. I've managed suppliers, teams, and hundreds of moving parts — often in front of rooms full of people who expect everything to be perfect.

Weddings are different, though. They're personal. So while I bring the same organizational rigour, I leave room for the moments that can't be scheduled — the ones you'll want to remember forever.

Based in Metro Manila, I work with couples who have done the planning and just need someone they can trust completely on the day.
